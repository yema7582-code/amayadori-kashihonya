# END B After the Rain

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: We're closing at the end of the month, as planned.

Minato: ...I see.

Shiori: My grandfather said to do as I see fit, but even if I do, I can't run it alone.

Shiori: Besides, I'm the one who decided to close. I want to see what I decided through to the end.

Minato: Yeah.

[show shiori sad]

Shiori: I'm only taking the window shelf home.

Minato: The scribbled one.

Shiori: The scribbled one.

[se waves]

Minato: (After that, I went to the shop a few more times before the end of the month. The packing was finished around noon on the last day.)

Minato: (The empty shelves looked smaller than I expected.)

[bg shop_night]
[bgm bgm_rain]

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: Four thousand two hundred and eleven books. All in boxes.

Minato: I packed half of them.

Shiori: A third.

Minato: You were counting?

Shiori: I was keeping the ledger.

Minato: (A used bookstore was apparently taking all the boxes. Only the window shelf would go to Shiori's room.)

[show shiori sad]

Shiori: Yesterday my grandfather talked about my mother, just a little.

Minato: What did he say?

Shiori: "She never borrowed the umbrella." Just that. Three times.

Minato: ...

Shiori: If he said it three times, I think he probably always wanted to lend it to her.

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: I left that umbrella in my grandfather's room. By the window of his hospital room.

Minato: Think he'll use it once he's discharged?

Shiori: I don't think so. He's someone who doesn't go out on rainy days.

Minato: Then why'd you leave it there?

Shiori: ...I wonder why.

Minato: (She said it the same way she did on the first day. But this time, I felt like I knew the reason.)

[bg seawall_eve]
[bgm bgm_ending]
[se waves]

Minato: (The evening of the closing day. The TV said the rainy season seemed to be over.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: It cleared up.

Minato: It did.

Shiori: There's no reason left to shelter from the rain.

Minato: (She didn't have the indigo umbrella with her today.)

Shiori: Shinomiya.

Minato: Hm?

Shiori: Thank you for coming to return the book.

Minato: It was my sister's book, though.

[show shiori smile]

Shiori: I know.

Shiori: ...If it rains again.

Minato: Yeah.

Shiori: I might be sheltering from the rain somewhere.

Minato: I'll look for you.

Shiori: And if you can't find me?

Minato: I'll look again the next time it rains.

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: ...You're a strange person.

Minato: I get that a lot.

[se waves]

Minato: (Shiori didn't wave. She gave a small bow and walked off along the seawall.)

Minato: (The sky was clear all the way to the horizon.)
