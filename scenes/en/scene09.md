# Scene 09 The Hospital Room

[bg hospital_room]
[bgm bgm_memory]

Minato: (The next afternoon. Shiomi General Hospital, fourth floor, the private room at the very end.)

Minato: (Outside the window, for once, it was sunny.)

Minato: (In front of the nurses' station, Shiori stopped for a moment.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: ...I will tell you now. My grandfather probably will not say anything.

Minato: Okay.

Shiori: He might get angry. Since I opened it without asking.

Minato: Then I'll get yelled at too.

Shiori: He will say you have nothing to do with it, Shinomiya.

Minato: Then I'll wait in the hall.

Shiori: ...Come. All the way in.

[hide shiori]

[se door_slide]

[show soichiro normal]

Soichiro: ...Who's that?

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: The one who returned the book with the letter. Shinomiya.

Minato: Hello.

Soichiro: ...I see.

Minato: (White hair. Round glasses. A gray vest—apparently worn on purpose over his hospital pajamas.)

Soichiro: Shinomiya. ...The elder daughter, then. The one who kept a book for ten years.

Minato: That's my sister. I'm sorry.

Soichiro: We don't charge late fees.

Shiori: I told him that, too.

Soichiro: ...I see.

Minato: (They say it the exact same way, I thought. Shiori didn't seem to notice.)

[show shiori sad]

Shiori: Grandpa. I opened the letter.

Soichiro: ...

Shiori: I'm going to read it.

Soichiro: I didn't ask you to.

Shiori: You didn't.

Minato: (Mr. Kuze turned toward the window. But he didn't cover his ears.)

[se paper]

Minato: (Shiori read it from the beginning, in the same voice she'd used at the shop.)

Minato: (From "Dear Dad" all the way to "please lend her the desk by the window.")

Minato: (Just once, partway through, Mr. Kuze's shoulders moved. It was at "a girl who doesn't take care of books.")

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: ...Haruka, it says.

Shiori: You said you didn't know the handwriting.

[show soichiro normal]

Soichiro: ...I did.

Shiori: Why?

Soichiro: I didn't know it.

Soichiro: Her handwriting in high school was far messier.

Shiori: ...

Soichiro: The second sheet—that hand I don't know. A grown woman's hand. I hadn't seen it in over ten years.

Minato: (Mr. Kuze slowly took off his glasses and wiped the lenses. They didn't look like they needed it.)

Soichiro: She was a girl who didn't take care of books.

Shiori: You told me that before.

Soichiro: She wrote in the books I lent her. In the margins. Next to the characters' lines, she'd add lines of her own. No matter how many times I told her, she wouldn't stop.

Soichiro: ...Thanks to her, half the books in the shop are unsellable.

[show shiori surprised]

Shiori: Half?

Soichiro: The shelf by the window. Every one of those has Haruka's scribbles in it.

Minato: (When we were packing, Shiori had told me "not yet" about the window shelf, and I'd left it alone.)

Shiori: ...You didn't throw them away.

Soichiro: As if I would.

[show soichiro gentle]

Soichiro: She was a girl who laughed a lot.

Soichiro: When it rained, she'd come running without an umbrella and take shelter in the shop. I'd tell her to go home; she wouldn't. I'd offer to lend her an umbrella; she wouldn't take it.

Soichiro: She said if she borrowed an umbrella, she'd have no reason left to shelter from the rain.

Minato: (I thought of the indigo umbrella. The one big enough for two.)

Soichiro: She named the shop, too.

Shiori: It's Kuze Lending Library, isn't it?

Soichiro: On the sign, yes. She called it "the shelter." "The Lending Library in the Rain." Told her friends so, too, apparently.

Soichiro: Even the school library lot started calling it that.

[show shiori sad]

Shiori: ...And my name, too.

Soichiro: I didn't know.

Soichiro: At the funeral, when I took you in, that's the first I heard your name. ...So. She named you in the shop.

Minato: (Mr. Kuze didn't take the letter. But he never took his eyes off the pages resting on Shiori's lap.)

[show soichiro normal]

Soichiro: Shinomiya.

Minato: Yes?

Soichiro: Books are meant to be brought back.

Minato: ...Yes.

Soichiro: And when you bring one back, you borrow another. That's how it works.

Soichiro: Close the shop, and that can't happen anymore.

[show shiori surprised]

Shiori: Grandpa.

Soichiro: Shiori. Do as you see fit. I can't stand behind that counter anymore.

Soichiro: ...Don't throw out the window shelf.

[se door_slide]

Minato: (When we left the room, we could see the sea from the hallway window.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: ..."Do as you see fit." That's the first time he's ever said that to me.

Minato: What does he usually say?

Shiori: "Do whatever you want."

Minato: Sounds similar.

Shiori: It's completely different.


Minato: (While we waited for the elevator, Shiori put the letter back in its envelope and tucked it into her apron—no, her uniform pocket.)

Shiori: I couldn't give it to him.

Minato: He didn't take it.

Shiori: ...But the whole time I was reading, he was listening.

Minato: I saw. His shoulders moved.

[show shiori smile]

Shiori: You notice all the wrong things, Shinomiya.

Minato: I get that a lot.

Shiori: From whom?

Minato: Chikage.

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: ...I see.

Minato: (The elevator doors opened. Shiori got in first, and before pressing "Close," she waited for me, just a little.)

[bgm stop]
