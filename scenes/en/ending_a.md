# END A Shiori's Bookmark

[label ending_A]

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: I've decided.

Minato: Decided what?

Shiori: Before we close, I'm going to open the shop for one more day. "The Last Lending Day."

Shiori: For the people on the shopping street, for our old customers, for everyone. ...And this time, I'll go tell them myself.

Minato: I'll help.

Shiori: I didn't ask you to.

Minato: I know.

[show shiori smile]

Shiori: I figured you'd say that, so I've already counted the flyers for two people.

[bg black]
[bgm bgm_ending]

Minato: (Ten days later. The last Saturday of the rainy season.)

[bg shop_rain]
[se door_bell]
[se rain_loop]

Minato: (It had been raining since morning. Shiori said it was perfect.)

Minato: (Umbrellas lined up outside the shop. The fishmonger's wife brought dried fish, and the woman from the flower shop said "Look how big you've gotten!" four times.)

Minato: (My sister came too. She bowed to Shiori and apologized for ten years' worth of overdue time. Shiori said "We don't charge late fees" three times.)

Minato: (On the desk by the window were the two sheets of the letter, framed. Beside them, a row of scribbled-in books. "Please feel free to read. No writing in the books.")

[show chikage smile]

Chikage: I brought a few people from the school library, too. To celebrate the partnership's comeback after twenty years.

Minato: It's only for one day.

Chikage: A comeback's a comeback, even for one day.

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: ...You're actually staying by her side, Minato. You didn't turn it into another turtle. Good job.

Minato: (That was all Chikage said before heading off toward the piles of books.)


Minato: (At the end of the day, Shiori got a phone call. No—to be exact, I listened in as she called the hospital.)

[se phone_ring]

[show soichiro gentle]

Soichiro: ...How many went out?

Shiori: A hundred and twenty. And lots of people came to see the window shelf.

Soichiro: ...I see.

Shiori: The fishmonger's wife cried. She said, "That's Haruka's handwriting."

Soichiro: She's always been quick to cry.

Shiori: Did you cry too, Grandpa?

Soichiro: I don't cry.

[show shiori smile]

Minato: (On the other end of the line, someone blew their nose. Shiori pretended not to hear.)

[hide soichiro]
[bg shop_night]
[bgm bgm_heart]

Minato: (Seven o'clock. The last customer left.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: Closing time.

Minato: Closing time.

Shiori: My grandfather called. "Don't throw out the shelf," again.

Minato: You're not going to, right?

Shiori: I'm taking it home. The whole window shelf. My room's going to get cramped.

[show shiori smile]

Shiori: And one more thing.

Minato: (Shiori took a piece of cardstock from her apron pocket.)

Minato: (A new checkout card, not yellowed at all. Stamped "Kuze Lending Library." In the name field, in straight handwriting—neither round nor slanted—"Minato Shinomiya.")

Minato: What's this?

Shiori: Your checkout card, Minato. The very last one.

Minato: What are you lending me?

[show shiori blush]

Shiori: ...Figure that out yourself.

Shiori: No due date.

Minato: (Outside, the rain eased a little.)

Minato: (In the umbrella stand, just one big indigo umbrella was left. The one bought for two.)
