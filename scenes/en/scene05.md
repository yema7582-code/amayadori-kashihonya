# Scene 05 Old Records in the School Library

[bg library]
[bgm bgm_mystery]
[se school_chime]

Minato: (The next day after school. In the back of the library, in front of the stacks room that's usually locked.)

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: Here. The old library committee rosters. About twenty years' worth.

Minato: ...How'd you know I was coming?

[if told_chikage -> s05_told]

Chikage: Obviously. You were walking down the shopping street under an umbrella yesterday, weren't you? With the lending library girl.

Minato: You were watching?

[show chikage pout]

Chikage: I happened to see. In front of the fish shop. ...So what are you digging into about that shop? You've been acting weird ever since I mentioned the partnership.

Minato: (No point dodging it now.)

Minato: ...I'm looking for someone who used to come to that shop. Someone who might've been the library's contact.

Chikage: Hmm. And that's something you can't tell me about?

Minato: Not right now.

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: ...Eh, fine. If you're looking at the rosters, you'll need the key to the stacks. I'll open it for you.

[jump s05_search]

[label s05_told]

Chikage: I told you I'd look into it, didn't I?

[show chikage smile]

Chikage: Also, I invited one more person today.

Minato: Huh?

[se door_slide]

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: ...Excuse me.

Minato: Shiori?

Shiori: Hayase called the shop. She said, "I'm pulling out the rosters, come over."

Chikage: The shop's phone number was in the ledger.

Minato: You just went ahead and...

Chikage: You're the one who said you were looking for whoever wrote the letter. It's faster with the person herself here.

[show shiori cold]

Shiori: I am not "the person herself." I just mind the shop.

Chikage: Okay, okay. Nice to meet you, Kuze. I'm Chikage Hayase. Minato and I go back to kindergarten.

Shiori: ...Kuze.

Minato: (Chikage was all smiles. Shiori, in another school's uniform, looked a little out of place.)

[label s05_search]

[se paper]

Minato: (The rosters were thin files bound by school year. Handwritten names, classes, assigned weekdays.)

Minato: (Ten to fifteen people per year. Twenty years' worth meant over two hundred.)

[if found_stamp -> s05_found]

Minato: All we've got is "a girl with round handwriting who was in charge"...

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: There are a hundred girls in here alone.

Minato: Yeah.

Chikage: If there was someone in charge of the partnership, isn't there a record of that?

Minato: There was nothing left at the shop.

Chikage: Hmm...

Minato: (I flipped through file after file. Plenty of names in round handwriting. None of them conclusive.)

Minato: (Just one page caught my eye. A school year from twenty-some years ago. Next to "Lending library (Kuze)" in the duty column, there was a single name.)

Minato: (An ink stain had spilled over that name. Only the last character was legible.)

Minato: "Haruka"...?

Chikage: Haruka?

Minato: Can't read the surname.

Chikage: There are a lot of girls named Haruka.

Minato: Yeah.

Minato: (I copied it into my notes anyway. Haruka. In charge of the lending library. Twenty-some years ago.)

[jump s05_end]

[label s05_found]

Minato: The card with the stamp was dated autumn, twenty-two years ago.

Minato: (I pulled out the file for that year.)

[show chikage surprised]

Chikage: Oh, here. The duty column says "Lending library (Kuze)."

Minato: So instead of a weekday shift, there was a contact person for the shop.

Chikage: And only one.

Minato: (I read the name. Round letters, slanting slightly right, in her own hand.)

Minato: "Haruka Minase."

[if told_chikage -> s05_shiori_hears]

Chikage: Minase, Haruka. ...Someone you know?

Minato: No.

Minato: (But I know this handwriting. It's the same as the envelope.)

Minato: (I have to tell Shiori this name.)

[jump s05_end]

[label s05_shiori_hears]

[show shiori surprised]

Shiori: ...

Minato: Shiori?

Shiori: Minase was my mother's surname before she married.

[show chikage surprised]

Chikage: What?

Shiori: Haruka is my mother's name.

Minato: (The air in the stacks suddenly felt colder.)

[show shiori sad]

Shiori: But my mother was supposed to be from Tokyo. I have never heard that she lived in this town.

Chikage: ...I'm sorry. I invited you without really thinking.

Shiori: No.

Shiori: If you had not invited me, I would never have known.

[label s05_end]

[se school_chime]

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: That's the final bell. I'm locking up the stacks.

Minato: Yeah.

Chikage: Hey, Minato.

Minato: Hm?

[show chikage sad]

Chikage: Never mind. ...I need to talk to you tomorrow, so give me a little time.

Minato: (With that, Chikage twirled the key around her finger and walked out ahead of me.)

[bgm stop]
[bg black]
