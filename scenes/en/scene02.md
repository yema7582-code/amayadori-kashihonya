# Scene 02 The Next Morning: Classroom to Library

[bg library]
[bgm bgm_daily]
[se school_chime]

Minato: (The next morning. The rain had stopped sometime during the night.)

Minato: (From first period to fourth, almost nothing on the blackboard sank in.)

Minato: (Round handwriting. An address slanting to the right. A postmark from ten years ago.)

Minato: (At lunch, I found myself in the school library. Nobody talks to you here.)

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: Found you.

Minato: ...How'd you know?

Chikage: When you vanish at lunch, it's either here or the roof. And the roof's soaked today.

Minato: What are you, a detective?

Chikage: Library committee. I've got counter duty today.

Minato: (Chikage leaned on the counter and started working through a pile of returned books.)

[show chikage smile]

Chikage: So?

Minato: "So" what?

Chikage: It's written all over your face. You've had that thinking face on since this morning.

Minato: Not really. I was thinking about the math quiz.

Chikage: You haven't worried about a quiz once since elementary school.

Minato: ...

Chikage: See?

Minato: (I couldn't argue. I've known Chikage since kindergarten, and lying to her has never once worked.)

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: Oh, right. You live over by the shopping arcade, don't you?

Minato: Behind it.

Chikage: Do you know "Kuze Lending Library"? The old place in the same row as the fish shop.

Minato: (My hands stopped.)

Minato: ...Yeah, I know it.

Chikage: I was sorting the stacks and found an old ledger. Apparently this library and that shop used to have a partnership.

Minato: A partnership?

Chikage: Looks like they'd borrow books we didn't have from that shop and lend them out to students. It was a long time ago, though. Even the teacher only remembered, like, "Oh, right, that was a thing."

Minato: Huh.

[show chikage surprised]

Chikage: ...That "huh" sounded weird.

Minato: It didn't.

Chikage: It did. When you're really not interested, you go "hmm."

Minato: (She's got me figured out down to that?)

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: What? Did something happen with that shop?

Minato: (I thought about the envelope from yesterday. And about the shop closing.)

Minato: (She asked me not to tell anyone about the closing. About the letter—she didn't say anything.)

[choice]
- Tell Chikage about the letter -> choice02_a
- Dodge the question -> choice02_b
[/choice]

[label choice02_a]
[flag told_chikage = 1]

Minato: ...Yesterday I ducked into that shop to get out of the rain.

Minato: I was returning a book my sister kept for ten years. And a letter fell out of it.

[show chikage surprised]

Chikage: A letter?

Minato: Still sealed. Addressed to the old man who runs the shop, postmarked ten years ago.

Chikage: Wait, what? That's basically a mystery case.

Minato: It's not a case.

Chikage: A letter that took ten years to arrive? That's totally a case.

[show chikage smile]

Chikage: So who's it from?

Minato: Didn't say. The handwriting was round, that's all.

Chikage: Round handwriting. Like, girly?

Minato: Probably.

Chikage: Hmm.

Minato: ...What?

Chikage: Nothing? Was there someone minding the shop?

Minato: Yeah. His granddaughter.

Chikage: Is she cute?

Minato: That's got nothing to do with it.

Chikage: You're not denying it.

Minato: (For the last ten minutes of lunch, Chikage flipped through the ledger, grinning the whole time.)

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: I'll dig a little more into the partnership records for you. I'll let you know if I find anything.

Minato: ...Thanks. That helps.

[jump scene02_end]

[label choice02_b]

Minato: My sister used to borrow books there, apparently. That's all.

[show chikage pout]

Chikage: That's all?

Minato: That's all.

Chikage: ...Fine, whatever.

Minato: (Chikage started scanning the barcodes on the returns a little harder than usual.)

[show chikage normal]

Chikage: If you don't want to tell me, that's fine. But you're bad at keeping secrets, you know.

Minato: I know.

Chikage: Then quit it.

Minato: (I didn't answer that.)

[label scene02_end]

[se school_chime]

Minato: (The warning bell rang. Chikage hugged her pile of books and disappeared into the stacks.)

Minato: (Outside the window, the clouds were gathering again.)

Minato: (After school, I'd go to that shop again. I could figure out a reason later.)

[bg black]
[se door_slide]

Minato: (On the way home, I texted my sister: "Why didn't you return that book ten years ago?")

Minato: (She replied three minutes later. "I thought I did." "Something happen?")

Minato: (I typed "there was an envelope in it," then deleted it. I sent "nothing.")

Minato: ("Is the old man there doing okay? He was scary but really kind.")

Minato: (I left it on read and stared at the screen for a while.)

[bgm stop]
[bg black]
