# Scene 04 Rain on the Shopping Street

[bg street_rain]
[bgm bgm_mystery]
[se rain_loop]

Minato: (Day three. The boxes were half done.)

Minato: (That day, Shiori flipped the shop sign to "Closed" and took off her apron.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: We are going to ask around.

Minato: Ask around.

Shiori: People who have been on this street for ten years might remember our regulars.

Minato: Like a cop show.

Shiori: A cop show would have more people in it.

Minato: (True. There were more shutters than shoppers.)

Minato: (Shiori opened an old indigo umbrella. It was huge, and one of its ribs was bent.)

[se umbrella_open]

Shiori: It is my grandfather's.

Minato: It's big.

Shiori: Apparently he bought it so two people could fit. I do not know who he meant to share it with.

Minato: (I put up my clear convenience-store umbrella and walked beside her.)

[se footsteps_wet]

Minato: (The old man at the futon shop remembered the lending library, but not its customers.)

Minato: (The tobacco shop had changed owners five years ago.)

Minato: (The woman at the flower shop looked at Shiori and said "Look how big you've gotten!" three times.)

[show shiori sad]

Shiori: ...Nothing so far.

Minato: You grew up here, Shiori?

Shiori: Since I was six.

Minato: And before that?

Shiori: Tokyo. I do not remember much.

Minato: (I didn't ask anything more.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: One more place.

Minato: Where?

Shiori: The fish shop. The owner's wife knows everything about this street.

[se footsteps_wet]

Fishmonger's wife: Well, if it isn't Shiori! It's been ages! How's your grandpa?

Shiori: He is fine. Bored.

Fishmonger's wife: Good to hear. And who's this? Your boyfriend?

[show shiori cold]

Shiori: No.

Minato: No.

Fishmonger's wife: My, you two are in sync.

Minato: (Shiori moved a little away from me.)

Shiori: About ten years ago, do you remember a customer who came to our shop a lot? A woman.

Fishmonger's wife: Ten years ago, hmm... We still had a fair number of customers back then.

Fishmonger's wife: Oh, but.

Shiori: But?

Fishmonger's wife: Not ten years ago—earlier. Way, way back. There was a girl who was always writing something in there. A high schooler.

Minato: Writing?

Fishmonger's wife: You know that little desk by the window? She'd sit there, and instead of reading, she'd be writing in a notebook. Soichiro would just watch her with that scary face, but he never chased her out.

Shiori: ...What was her name?

Fishmonger's wife: Oh, I couldn't tell you her name. She laughed a lot, that girl. That's the only time I ever saw Soichiro talk so much.

[show shiori surprised]

Shiori: My grandfather... talked?

Fishmonger's wife: He did! Can't picture it now, can you?

Fishmonger's wife: Then one day she stopped coming. Graduated, I suppose. Soichiro was down in the dumps for a while.

Fishmonger's wife: ...Oh, listen to me, going on about the old days. Here, take this. For your grandpa.

Minato: (A plastic bag of dried horse mackerel was pushed into Shiori's arms.)

[bgm bgm_rain]

Minato: (On the way back, the rain got heavier.)

[se rain_heavy]

Minato: (My clear umbrella flapped and bucked in the wind.)

[se thunder_far]

Minato: Ah.

Minato: (Two ribs flipped inside out at once.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: ...Well, it was three hundred yen.

Minato: Five hundred.

Shiori: Same thing.

Minato: (Without a word, Shiori tilted the indigo umbrella a little toward me.)

Minato: (An umbrella bought for two. And two of us did fit. Barely.)

Minato: (Keeping our shoulders from touching was harder than I thought.)

[choice]
- "I'll hold the umbrella." -> choice04_a
- Walk on without saying anything -> choice04_b
[/choice]

[label choice04_a]
[flag trust_shiori +1]

Minato: I'll hold the umbrella.

[show shiori surprised]

Shiori: Huh?

Minato: Your wrist's all bent. You'll get tired holding it like that.

Shiori: ...Just because you are tall.

Minato: If the short one holds it, the ribs poke the tall one in the head.

[show shiori smile]

Shiori: Heh.

Minato: (I felt like it was the first time I'd seen her really laugh.)

Shiori: Then, please.

Minato: (The handle was colder than I expected.)

Minato: (Shiori hugged the bag of fish to her chest and stepped half a step closer.)

Shiori: Your shoulder is getting wet, Shinomiya.

Minato: Comes with the job of umbrella holder.

Shiori: Where do you learn things like that?

Minato: My sister.

Shiori: Your sister teaches you a lot. Even if she does not return books.

[jump scene04_end]

[label choice04_b]

Minato: (I started to say something, then didn't.)

Minato: (Shiori kept her eyes forward, gripping the umbrella handle with both hands.)

Minato: (Every now and then the bag of fish bumped against my arm.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: ...

Minato: ...

Minato: (It was a silence that wasn't quite awkward.)

[label scene04_end]

[bg shop_rain]
[se door_bell]

Minato: (When we got to the shop, Shiori closed the umbrella and tried to straighten the bent rib with her fingers. It didn't work.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: "She laughed a lot."

Minato: Yeah.

Shiori: I cannot imagine words like that coming out of my grandfather's mouth.

Minato: But she said she saw it.

Shiori: ...Maybe I do not know anything about my grandfather.

[bgm stop]
[bg black]
