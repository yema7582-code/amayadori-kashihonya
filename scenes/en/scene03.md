# Scene 03 The Lending Library, Second Visit

[bg street_rain]
[bgm bgm_rain]
[se rain_loop]

Minato: (After school. Right on forecast, it started raining again.)

Minato: (Today I'd bought an umbrella at the convenience store. One of the clear plastic ones.)

[se door_slide]
[se door_bell]
[bg shop_rain]

[show shiori surprised]

Shiori: ...

Minato: Hey.

[show shiori cold]

Shiori: Did you keep other books, too?

Minato: No.

Shiori: Then are you sheltering from the rain? You have an umbrella.

Minato: I do.

Shiori: ...

Minato: I thought I'd help. If you're closing, there must be packing and stuff.

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: I did not ask you to.

Minato: I know.

Minato: (Shiori was quiet for a moment, then pointed to the back of the shop.)

Shiori: The back shelves, third row down and below. Everything goes into boxes. Spines facing up, in numerical order.

Minato: Got it.

Shiori: Please do not touch them with wet hands.

Minato: I dried them.

Shiori: And the umbrella goes outside.

Minato: ...Yes, ma'am.

[se footsteps_wet]

Minato: (The work was plainer than I expected. Pull a book, check the number, put it in the box. Over and over.)

Minato: (Shiori sat by the register, silently writing in a ledger. Just the sound of her pen and the rain.)

Minato: What are these numbers?

Shiori: The order the books came in. My grandfather wrote every one of them by hand.

Minato: Every one?

Shiori: Every one. Four thousand two hundred and eleven books.

Minato: That's amazing.

Shiori: His handwriting is terrible, so about half of them are unreadable.

Minato: (I couldn't tell whether that was a joke.)

Minato: Your grandpa's not here today?

[show shiori sad]

Shiori: He is in the hospital. Since spring.

Minato: ...I see.

Shiori: His heart. The surgery is done, and it is not life or death. But he cannot work the shop anymore.

Minato: So that's why you're closing.

Shiori: I cannot run it on my own.

Minato: (The sound of the pen had stopped.)

Shiori: I took the letter to the hospital yesterday.

Minato: Did you give it to him?

Shiori: I showed it to him. Only the address.

Minato: What did he say?

Shiori: "I don't know that handwriting."

Minato: ...

Shiori: That is all he said. Then he turned toward the window.

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: So I still have it.

Minato: (Shiori pulled just the corner of the envelope out of her apron pocket to show me.)

Minato: Then why not find out who wrote it?

Shiori: And what would that accomplish?

Minato: If it turns out he does know the handwriting, he might take it.

[show shiori surprised]

Shiori: ...

Minato: If it's postmarked ten years ago, maybe it's someone who came to the shop back then. Don't you have records or something?

Shiori: There are checkout cards. We kept the card for every book that was returned.

Minato: Every one?

Shiori: My grandfather is not the type to throw things away.

[se paper]

Minato: (Shiori brought three shoeboxes from the back. They were crammed with yellowed cardstock cards.)

Minato: (Dates, names, and return stamps. The bundle from ten years ago alone had over a hundred.)

Shiori: I will narrow it down to the rainy season ten years ago. The postmark is June.

Minato: Wonder if my sister's name is in there.

Shiori: Shinomiya. ...Here. The same week.

Minato: (My sister's handwriting was as awful then as it is now.)

Minato: (We split the rest of the cards between us and started flipping through them.)

[label choice03_a]
[flag found_stamp = 1]

Minato: (Just looking at the names on the front wasn't getting us anywhere. I flipped one over.)

Minato: (Most were blank. But a few had a faint, round vermilion stamp on them.)

Minato: What's this stamp?

[show shiori surprised]

Shiori: ..."Shiomi High School Library."

Minato: That's my school.

Shiori: They had a partnership. The shop used to lend books to the school library.

Minato: I heard about that today.

Shiori: From whom?

Minato: A friend on the library committee.

Minato: (I lined up the stamped cards. On one of the old ones, dated much further back, was handwriting I recognized.)

Minato: This one.

Shiori: "School library order — in charge:" ...The name is smudged.

Minato: But this handwriting.

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: ...Round. Slanting to the right.

Minato: Same as the envelope.

Shiori: This card is more than twenty years old.

Minato: Yeah.

Shiori: The person who wrote a letter ten years ago came here even before that...?

Minato: And as the school library's contact, no less.

Minato: (Shiori laid the card next to the envelope and looked at them for a long time.)

[jump scene03_end]

[label choice03_b]

Minato: Let me see the envelope for a sec.

Shiori: Do not open it.

Minato: I won't.

Minato: (The address: "Mr. Kuze." Short flicks on the strokes, all round. The final dot weirdly big.)

Minato: I think it's a woman's handwriting. Someone young.

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: Is that so?

Minato: One of my sister's friends wrote like this. The type who draws hearts in the margins of her notebook.

Shiori: That is a stereotype.

Minato: Probably.

Minato: (In the end, the bundles of cards gave us no more clues than that.)

[label scene03_end]

[se rain_loop]

Minato: (Before I knew it, it had gotten dark outside.)

[show shiori normal]

Shiori: It is seven o'clock.

Minato: Closing time?

Shiori: Closing time.

Minato: Can I come back tomorrow?

Shiori: ...There are still boxes left.

Minato: Is that a yes?

[show shiori cold]

Shiori: As long as you do not touch anything with wet hands.

[bgm stop]
[bg black]
